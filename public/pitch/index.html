Borehole log · the solution

Every probe gets its own clean room.

Paste a public repo, a failing test and the last good ref. Each probe is a fresh Nebius Serverless AI Job that installs that commit’s own dependencies.

Field notes · the benchmark

Right both times. Slower both times.

Faultline is not a faster bisect. It is the fresh-environment harness — run on Nebius, both ends proven, the culprit explained.

Faultline · v0.2.0Sheet 08 of 10
On the drift fixture the reused environment is wrong ten times out of ten; a fresh venv per step is right, and so is Faultline. On a real attrs regression with no drift everything is right. Faultline is slower on both — about four minutes of cold start per probe, so every round costs minutes. It is the harness you would otherwise write.
